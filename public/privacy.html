Privacy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Spin the Fortune is a free game. It needs no account, no password and no email address, and it uses no advertising or tracking.

What we store

  • Rooms. When you start a game we store the room code, the puzzle phrase, the category, the player names and scores you typed, and a one-way hash of your room key (never the key itself). A room is deleted automatically after 24 hours without use.
  • Anonymous statistics. Counters such as "rooms created" or "puzzles solved", grouped by day, language and country. They contain no personal data and cannot be traced back to you.
  • Abuse protection. To limit spam we keep a short-lived hash of your IP address, never the address itself. It is deleted automatically after a short time.
  • Shared phrases (optional). Only if you tick "Share my new phrases with the community" (off by default), the phrases you wrote are sent anonymously, together with their category and the page language, with no link to you, your room or your device. After review by us they may appear in the ready-made phrases offered to other players, and you can ask us to remove one using the questions page. Do not include personal names, song lyrics or long quotes.
  • Questions and suggestions. If you write to us we store your message, its type, the language and country, and your email address only if you choose to give it. Your email is never shown publicly and we do not send emails at the moment. Messages and answers are kept until we delete them; you can ask us to delete yours using your ticket code.

On your device

We use no cookies. The page saves a few settings in your browser (local storage): language, sound on/off, the key of your current room, the codes of your messages and the settings you chose. You can erase them from your browser settings at any time.

Donations

The game is free and donations are voluntary. If you choose to donate, you are taken to Ko-fi (ko-fi.com), an external service with its own privacy policy: payment details are handled by Ko-fi and its payment providers, never by this site, and we do not receive them on this site. Donating gives no extra features.

Who processes the data

The site runs on Cloudflare (hosting, database and delivery), which processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the pages and protect them from attacks. We do not sell data and do not share it with anyone else.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data linked to a message you sent, by writing to us from the questions page with your ticket code. Room data disappears on its own after 24 hours. If you are in the EU you may also complain to your national data protection authority.

Children

The game is meant for families. Do not type full names or other personal details of children as player names: first names or nicknames are enough.

Contact

Use the Questions and suggestions page.
