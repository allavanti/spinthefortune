How it works

  1. 1

    Start a game on your phone

    Tap Start a game. You get a screen code with 6 characters and a ready-made set of phrases to try.

  2. 2

    Open the screen on a TV or PC

    Go to this site/screen and type the code. On a PC, cast the Chrome tab to your TV from the browser menu.

  3. 3

    Spin and call letters

    Tap Spin: the wheel turns on the big screen. Then call a consonant: each one found scores the wheel value. Buy a vowel, solve, and the scores are kept for you.

Game rules

  • Goal. Reveal the hidden phrase by calling letters. Whoever solves it keeps the points of the round.
  • Your turn. Spin the wheel. If it lands on a value, call a consonant: every time it appears in the phrase you earn that value (a 300 and three letters found is 900).
  • Found one? You keep the turn. Spin again, buy a vowel, or try to solve.
  • Vowels cost points. 250 by default, and you need that many points in the round. You pay even if the vowel is not in the phrase. The vowels are A, E, I, O and U.
  • A miss passes the turn. A consonant or a vowel that is not in the phrase sends the turn to the next player.
  • Bankrupt wipes the points of your round and passes the turn. Lose a turn passes the turn and keeps your points.
  • Solving. Say the phrase out loud. If it is right, tap Solved: the points of your round go into your total and the whole phrase is revealed. If it is wrong, tap Wrong and the turn passes.
  • Rounds and totals. Round points restart with every new phrase; totals stay until you reset them in Settings.
  • Make your own rules. In Settings change the wheel values, how many Bankrupt and Lose a turn it has, and the price of a vowel. With no player names you play without scores.

Tips for a good game

  • Write 15 to 30 phrases first. One per line, as Category | PHRASE. The category is shown on screen and helps players.
  • Keep phrases under about 40 letters. The board stays readable from the sofa.
  • Accents are fine. Players call E and it also finds È.
  • Add player names. They switch on scores and the wheel. The current player is highlighted on screen. Up to 12 names.
  • Want sound? Click once on the screen page, because browsers only allow audio after a click. Press M to mute.
  • Casting from a PC? In Chrome open the menu, choose Cast, then Cast tab, and keep that tab open.
  • Playing with friends far away? Send them the screen link from the host page. They see the same board on their own device.

Good to know

  • Free, with no sign-up. Works in current browsers on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Scores follow the classic rules: spin, call a consonant for the wheel value, buy a vowel, bankrupt, lose a turn, solve. Wheel values and vowel cost are yours to change in Settings.
  • Your phrases and players stay in your browser. The screen only receives the letters already revealed, so nobody can read the answer from the page.
  • Rooms are anonymous and are deleted after 24 hours without activity.
  • We count anonymous totals, such as rooms created and letters called, by language and country. We do not collect names or emails.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is nothing to pay and no account to create.

Do I need a physical wheel?

No. The wheel is digital and spins on the screen, with sound. Without player names the game runs without scores.

Which TVs work?

Any TV that can show a web page or receive a cast from Chrome: smart TVs with a browser, Chromecast, Google TV, or a laptop on an HDMI cable.

Can players see the answer on the screen?

No. The screen is sent the phrase with the hidden letters replaced, so the answer stays on the host device.

What happens if my phone locks or goes offline?

The board keeps its last state. When the phone is back online it sends the current state again.

Can I use it in a classroom?

Yes. Use categories as topics, add the class as players, and project the board. Nothing personal is stored on our side.
