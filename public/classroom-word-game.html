Spin the Fortune

A free word game for the classroom

Spin the Fortune shows a phrase puzzle board on your projector or interactive whiteboard while you run the game from a phone or tablet. Write your own phrases for the lesson. Students need no accounts and nothing is installed.

Start a game

What teachers use it for

  • Vocabulary and idioms. Turn this week's words into puzzles. Categories on screen give students a hint.
  • Spelling and letter patterns. Students call letters and see where they fall, which is good practice for spelling and word shape.
  • Language learners. Proverbs, idioms and everyday sentences work well for ESL and for foreign-language classes. The ready-made catalog is available in English and Italian, separately.
  • Revision games. Split the class into teams. Use the scoring wheel, or turn scores off and just race to solve.

Why it suits a school setting

  • No student accounts, no emails, no app to install.
  • The screen only receives the letters already revealed, so no one can read the answer from the page.
  • No advertising and no tracking. Only anonymous totals are counted.
  • Your phrases and team names stay in your own browser.

Quick start

  1. On your phone or tablet tap Start a game and note the screen code.
  2. On the classroom computer open spinthefortune.com/screen, type the code and put the tab on the projector.
  3. Paste your phrases, one per line as Category | PHRASE, add team names and start.
