Spin the Fortune

A word game for your TV

Spin the Fortune is a free puzzle game made for the big screen: the board and the wheel appear on the TV, and the host controls everything from a phone. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

Start a game

Ways to get the board on the TV

  • Smart TV with a browser: open spinthefortune.com/screen and type the code.
  • Chromecast or Google TV: in Chrome on a PC choose Cast, then Cast tab, and keep the screen tab open.
  • Laptop on an HDMI cable: open the screen page and put the browser in full screen.

What you get on screen

  • A large puzzle board that reveals letters as they are called, with the category as a hint.
  • A spinning wheel with sound, current player highlighted, and running scores.
  • Optional strip of missed letters, which you can hide in Settings.
  • The phrase is masked on the server, so the answer never reaches the TV.

Run it from the phone

Tap Start a game, enter the code on the TV, then spin, call letters, buy vowels and mark the phrase as solved. You can change the wheel values and the vowel price in Settings, or use ready-made phrases by category and difficulty.
